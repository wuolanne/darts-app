DartsFlow - Checkout Practice Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-24

DartsFlow - Checkout Practice is a local-only darts practice app. This privacy policy explains what data the app uses and what it does not collect.

What the app does not collect

  • The app does not require a user account.
  • The app does not collect personal information.
  • The app does not share personal information.
  • The app does not use advertising.
  • The app does not use analytics.
  • The app does not use cloud sync.
  • The app does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photo library.

What the app stores locally

Practice history, settings, language selection and other local app preferences are stored only on your own device by using local app storage.

This local data is used only to make the app work, for example to save your checkout practice history, Around the Clock session history, local personal bests and app settings.

Data deletion

If the app is removed from your device, or if the app’s local storage is cleared, your local training history and saved settings may be lost.

Future changes

If analytics, crash reporting, ads, login or cloud sync are added in a future release, this privacy policy will be updated before that release is published.
